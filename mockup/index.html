FAQ

Everything
you might ask.

How much does condo cleaning cost in Toronto?

VeroCleaning starts at $120 for a regular condo clean. Pricing is fixed and published — you see the full amount before booking. Deep cleans from $180, move-out from $216.

How do you vet your cleaners?

Every cleaner is background-checked and reference-checked before their first job. If something is missed, we come back and re-clean it free within 24 hours.

How do I book a cleaning?

Pick your service above, see your fixed price, choose a time, and confirm. The whole thing takes about 60 seconds — no phone calls needed.

What if I’m not satisfied?

Our 24-hour guarantee means if something was missed, we come back and make it right at no extra cost.

Do I need to provide supplies?

No — our pros arrive with all the supplies and equipment needed for your clean.

Do you also clean offices and commercial spaces?

Yes. Business spaces are quoted on a written scope instead of a fixed online price, because the work depends on square footage and frequency.

Do you work after hours?

For offices, clinics and retail we clean after hours and on weekends, so your business never stops. Home visits run seven days a week, 10am–5pm.

Which areas do you cover?

Toronto and the wider GTA: Downtown, Liberty Village, CityPlace, Fort York, Yorkville, The Annex, Leslieville, The Beaches, Midtown, North York, Scarborough, Etobicoke and East York, plus Mississauga, Vaughan, Concord, Markham, Richmond Hill, Brampton, Oakville and Burlington.
